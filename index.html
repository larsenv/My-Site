About Me

I taught myself HTML at 11 and Python at 13, mostly out of curiosity about how things worked under the hood. That curiosity turned into reverse engineering proprietary file formats and network protocols, which is how most of my early projects got started.

Most people who know my name know it from Wii related work, and that is a fair reputation since I have spent well over a decade in that space. But the skills behind it, reverse engineering, backend development, server infrastructure, and archiving, apply well beyond just one console. Wii preservation is simply where I happened to start.

Larsen Davis Vallecillo was born on December 8, 2001, and grew up in Texas. At 13 years old, he created RiiConnect24, a project that revived the Wii's discontinued network services including Wii Mail, the News Channel, Forecast Channel, Everybody Votes Channel, Check Mii Out Channel, and Nintendo Channel, along with Animal Crossing City Folk DLC, RiiTag, SysCheck, the Banner Repository, the GeckoCodes Mirror, the RiiConnect24 Bot, and the Spiffy360 Archive. He led the project for eight and a half years, longer than the original Nintendo service it replaced was ever online, before stepping away in December 2023 and passing the project on to WiiLink.

Beyond RiiConnect24, Larsen has contributed to GameTDB, Wiimmfi, GlowWii, the MKW Hack Pack, and the TiVoDatabase ecosystem, including its Account Checker, Restore Image Archive, and Wiki. His independent projects include Nick Jr Videos and More and its supporting media stack, PrintMiiOut, PictoCard, Discross, RiiTube, and PokéWii, along with specialized downloaders built for services like YouTube TV, Hoopla, and Kanopy.

His archival work includes contributions to ArchiveTeam, Redump, WiiMart, WiiLink, the Open Shop Channel, and the Wii and DSi Shop Channel preservation efforts. He has also built tools such as the My Pokemon Ranch Platinum Project, the Pokemon Mystery Dungeon WiiWare Patch, Animal Crossing City Folk Deluxe, Mii Renderer by PF2M, Spriter Resource, TrimmerBot, Wizarr Invite Bot, Globe Spinner for the Browser, Every Character From Everything.com, and Why Not Take a Break.com.

Larsen modded his first Wii in 2012 and is still actively building and maintaining projects today, making him one of the more prolific contributors to Wii preservation, archival, modding, development, and research in the community's history.
