About Me

I've had an interest in computers since I was young. I taught myself HTML at 11 and Python at 13, mostly out of curiosity about how things worked under the hood. That curiosity turned into reverse engineering proprietary file formats and network protocols, which is how most of my early projects got started.

Most people who know my name know it from Wii related work, and that is a fair reputation since I have spent well over a decade in that space. But the skills behind it, reverse engineering, backend development, server infrastructure, and archiving, apply well beyond just one console. Wii preservation is simply where I happened to start.

At 13 years old, I created RiiConnect24, a project that revived the Wii's discontinued network services including Wii Mail, the News Channel, Forecast Channel, Everybody Votes Channel, Check Mii Out Channel, and Nintendo Channel, along with many other pieces of infrastructure like RiiTag, a Discord bot, and a whole community fanbase that grew up around it. I led the project for eight and a half years, longer than the original Nintendo service it replaced was ever online, before stepping away in December 2023 and passing it on to WiiLink.

I'm still actively building and maintaining projects today, and I consider myself one of the more prolific contributors to Wii preservation, archival, modding, development, and research in the community's history. That said, my work these days isn't limited to the Wii space. I'm always picking up new projects wherever my interest takes me.

Outside of my project work, I keep things fairly simple. I mostly spend my free time on whatever passion project has my attention at the moment, since that is genuinely how I like to unwind. I enjoy chatting with friends online, and every so often I'll play a video game.

Discross

Bridges Discord to older hardware and legacy browsers. I forked the original project and became the lead maintainer, building out new features and hosting for full backwards compatibility.

discross.net

PictoCard

A free digital greeting card service. Upload a photo and a message, then send it by email, Discord, or straight to a Wii console through WiiLink.

pictocard.net

PokéWii

A fan translation team producing English patches for Japanese WiiWare Pokémon games, including Pokémon Mystery Dungeon WiiWare and My Pokémon Ranch: Platinum Edition.

pokewii.net

RiiTube

A video streaming platform that lets the Wii watch YouTube content. Originally part of RiiConnect24, I now independently host and maintain it, with meaningful usage in regions where smart TVs are less common.

riitube.net
